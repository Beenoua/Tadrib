Part 1: Are You Eligible?

Before applying, you must meet specific educational and professional requirements set by PMI. This is the first filter where many fail.

Category 1: Four-Year Degree

  • Bachelor's degree or global equivalent.
  • 36 months of unique non-overlapping professional project management experience.
  • 35 Contact Hours of Project Management Education.

Category 2: High School Diploma

  • High school diploma or associate's degree.
  • 60 months of unique non-overlapping professional project management experience.
  • 35 Contact Hours of Project Management Education.

The "35 Contact Hours" Requirement

This is mandatory. You cannot self-study for this. You must attend a formal training course. At Tadrib.ma, our training not only provides these hours but bridges the gap between theory and the Moroccan market reality.

What if I get Audited?

The audit is random. It's not a penalty. If selected, you have 90 days to mail your materials to PMI.

  • Copies of your diploma/degree.
  • Signatures from your supervisors verifying your experience.
  • Certificate of 35 contact hours (Provided by Tadrib.ma).
